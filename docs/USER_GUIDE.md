# Daily guide for the boutique

## Taking a new order (about 1 minute)
1. Tap **New order**.
2. Type the customer's name or phone. If new, tap **New** and enter name and mobile.
3. For each item, choose a service (price fills in), adjust qty/price/discount. Tap **Add another item** for more.
4. Set **Expected delivery**, who will do the work, and any notes.
5. In **Quick billing**, tick **Create the invoice now** and enter the **Advance received**.
6. Tap **Save & create invoice**. The share screen opens.

## Sending the invoice on WhatsApp
1. Tap **Create secure link** (optional, lets the customer open the invoice online).
2. Tap **Download PDF**.
3. Tap **Open WhatsApp** → check the message → tap 📎 → Document → choose the PDF → **Send**.
The app cannot attach the PDF or send for you. It only fills in the message.

## Through the day
- Move the order along: **In progress → Quality check → Ready**. When marked Ready, the app offers a "ready for collection" WhatsApp message.
- **Record payment** when the balance is paid. Use **Refund** when giving money back.
- **Use material** on an item to take thread, stones or fabric out of stock against that order.
- When it leaves: **Delivered** (or record a Rapido/courier booking under Delivery).

## Fixing mistakes
- Wrong amount on an invoice → **Cancel invoice** (give a reason) → **Edit** the order → **Issue invoice** again.
- Payment entered twice → tap ↩ next to it → **Reverse** (give a reason).
- Material used by mistake → **Return** on that item.

## Owner only
- **Job cost** on each order: enter labour and other costs, tick **Done**. Profit becomes "actual" when all items are done.
- **Reports**: profit, collections, who owes money, stock value. **Export CSV** for your accountant.
- **Settings → Staff & access**: invite staff and choose exactly what they can do. Keep "See costs, margins and profit" for yourself unless needed.
